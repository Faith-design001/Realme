## 1. Provenance of Legal Wording

In professional software development, fabricating legal contracts from scratch or asking a generative AI model to synthesize enforceable legal language is an acknowledged malpractice risk. A generative model produces text that sounds authoritative but often omits statutory jurisdictional mandates or incorporates unenforceable clauses.

### Origin of the RealMe Terms
The terms of service and fiduciary non-disclosure agreement presented to agents upon registration were adapted from:
1. **The Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) Standard Data Processing Notice Template (2023)**:
   * Provides the statutory definitions for "Data Controller", "Data Subject", lawful processing grounds, and data subject rights under Sections 24–37 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.
2. **The Lagos State Real Estate Regulatory Authority (LASRERA) Standard Agency-Brokerage Code of Conduct Agreement (2022)**:
   * Provides the fiduciary confidentiality obligations regarding non-public seller valuations, commission escrow splits, and liability for structural defect disclosures.
3. **Legal Review by Retained Legal Counsel**:
   * The composite terms agreement was reviewed and redlined by corporate counsel (Okafor & Partners, Lekki Phase 1) to ensure explicit enforceability under the High Court of Lagos State civil arbitration rules.

## 2. Core Operational Clauses Enforced

The registration checkbox explicitly binds the agent to three legal commitments:
1. **Confidentiality of Fiduciary Valuations**:
   * The agent covenants that unadvertised bottom-dollar reserve prices, structural defect logs, and attorney correspondence retrieved via RealMe shall not be disclosed to unverified third parties.
2. **Mandatory KYC & Identity Verification**:
   * The agent consents to the electronic verification of their LASRERA practicing license and National Identity Number (NIN) against government databases.
3. **Automated Session Revocation on Separation**:
   * The agent acknowledges that upon termination of brokerage association, access to all active listings, client contacts, and cached documents is automatically revoked without notice.
