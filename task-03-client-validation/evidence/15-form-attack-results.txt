[FORM-LEVEL ATTACK & DEFEAT TEST RESULTS]

1. Single-Character Name Attack:
   - Input: "a"
   - Result: BLOCKED. Form displayed caution SVG icon with error: "Full legal name must contain at least two words separated by a space." Submit button remained in aria-disabled="true" state.

2. Whitespace-Only Password Attack:
   - Input: "            " (12 space characters)
   - Result: DEFEATED CLIENT RULE. Because the password length check measured raw string length (12) without trim() on initial draft, the browser briefly treated 12 spaces as valid!

3. Minimal Syntax Email Attack:
   - Input: "a@b"
   - Result: BLOCKED. RFC regex required standard top-level domain (e.g. .com, .ng). Error displayed in real-time.

4. Copied Trailing Space in Email Attack:
   - Input: "agent@realme.ng "
   - Result: DEFEATED REAL-TIME CHECK. Trailing space caused regex match to fail until user blurred or trimmed.

5. Form Bypass Attempt via Disabled Button:
   - User clicked disabled button without accepting terms:
   - Result: Form did not submit; accessible alert fired: "You must complete all required fields and accept fiduciary terms to register."
