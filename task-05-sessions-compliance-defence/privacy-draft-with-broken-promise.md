# RealMe Privacy Notice (Draft Version with Broken Promise)

**Brokerage**: Realme Properties Ltd (Lekki, Lagos)  
**Jurisdiction**: Lagos State, Federal Republic of Nigeria (NDPA 2023)  

---

## 1. What Data We Collect
We collect your full legal name, work email address, LASRERA practitioner license code, and encrypted password. During your platform usage, we record transaction inquiries, structural caveat notes, and session IP timestamps.

---

## 2. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure
You may request account deletion at any time through your Profile Security settings.

### Our Promises to You:
1. **Grace Period**: Your account enters a 7-day deactivation grace period during which you can cancel deletion.
2. **[THE BROKEN PROMISE]**:
   > *"Upon requesting account deletion, all property deal notes, client valuation inquiries, and structural inspection correspondence previously submitted by the agent will be immediately purged from all brokerage intelligence logs and search databases."*
3. **Identity Anonymization**: Your personal name and email will be purged from the active user registry.

---

## 3. How a Promise Like This Gets Written in the First Place
A promise like this gets written when a software developer or product manager conflates an individual's personal identity with the collaborative business records they created. The developer assumes that complying with the "Right to Erasure" means deleting every database row associated with `agentId`, completely blind to the fact that deleting structural defect caveats destroys the permanent legal disclosure trail for property buyers and violates real estate conveyancing law.
